## 5. Important Business Findings

1. **Severe Category Margin Imbalance:** While Technology and Office Supplies maintain healthy margins (~17.2%–17.5%), Furniture lags by roughly 14.6 to 14.8 percentage points, returning only 2.61% margin despite generating over $754k in sales.
2. **High-Volume Drag in Loss-Making Lines:** Tables alone generated over $208k in sales while losing $17,753.21. Significant sales volume is currently dedicated to value-destroying lines.
3. **January Seasonality / Vulnerability:** Historical lows in both profitability (January 2024 at -$3,189.80) and sales growth (January 2025 at -75.07%) have occurred in the month of January.
4. **Discount Exposure:** The business maintains an average discount rate of 15.54%. A high average discount rate coincides with product lines operating at negative margins.
